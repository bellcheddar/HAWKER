Privacy Policy

HAWKER for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Vision Pro and Apple Watch. Last updated 28 August 2026.

HAWKER collects nothing.

No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifier, no crash reporting, no telemetry of any kind. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer, and there is no server belonging to HAWKER for it to be sent to.

What the app stores

  • A cache of the public scientific records it has downloaded, held in the app's own caches directory on your device.
  • Nothing else. There is no user profile, no document store and no cloud sync.

Deleting the app removes the cache with it.

Where the app connects

HAWKER builds its working set from public, freely available scientific databases. It makes outbound HTTPS requests only to these services:

  • EMBL-EBI: ChEMBL and UniChem
  • ClinicalTrials.gov (US National Library of Medicine)
  • Open Targets Platform
  • RCSB Protein Data Bank
  • PubChem (US National Institutes of Health)
  • openFDA (US Food and Drug Administration)

These requests carry no personal information. They contain only the scientific identifier being looked up (for example a ChEMBL compound id or a PDB entry code) and a descriptive User-Agent naming the app so those services can identify the traffic. As with any internet request, the operators of those services will see the connecting IP address under their own privacy policies, which are linked above.

On-device processing

HAWKER classifies the free-text reason a clinical trial gave for stopping. That runs entirely on your device, on the Apple Neural Engine, using a model built into the operating system. No text is uploaded for processing and there is no external inference service.

Children

HAWKER is a research reference tool for scientists. It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Not medical advice

HAWKER makes no diagnostic or treatment claim and gives no medical advice. It is a browser over public research databases. The freedom-to-operate figure it shows is a crude estimate computed from public dates, is labelled as an estimate throughout the app, and is not legal guidance.

Changes
